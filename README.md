## About Me

Most first-year CS students are showing you toy projects. This isn't that.

I'm building [Holkam Advisory](https://holkamadvisory.com), a regulatory intelligence firm that tells South African law firms what's coming out of Australia twelve to eighteen months before it reaches them. Not a class assignment — a live pipeline shipping real intelligence briefs to real firms, run by a first-year Computer Science student at the University of Adelaide while my coursework is still teaching me the fundamentals underneath it.

South African firms aren't behind because they lack talent. They're behind because almost nobody is doing the work of translating what's already happened elsewhere into what's about to happen here.

Also building websites for eCommerce clients outside of Holkam — references available on request.
