## What I'm building

**[Holkam Advisory](https://holkamadvisory.com)** — a monthly regulatory intelligence licence for small and mid-sized South African law firms.

- A multi-agent Claude Code pipeline generating firm-specific intelligence reports
- A Notion-integrated analytics layer tracking engagement, connections, and content performance
- A self-improving research loop — daily AU/SA regulatory pulls and weekly performance data feed lessons back into every future brief automatically

The thesis: South African firms aren't behind because they lack talent. They're behind because almost nobody is doing the work of translating what's already happened elsewhere into what's about to happen here.
